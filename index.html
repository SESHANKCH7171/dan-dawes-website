The Journey

Two decades of building from Charm City.

Dan Dawes is a Baltimore-born entrepreneur and digital marketing veteran who believes technology only works when it serves real human relationships.

From launching Congruent Media on the second floor of The Natty Boh Tower in 2003 and steering it to an acquisition by Havas, to co-founding Response Labs in 2014 and returning full-circle to Brewer’s Hill, his career is defined by operational rigor, people-first leadership, and a builder’s instinct.

“Being the entrepreneur that I am, that’s really all the fuel that I needed and I started taking it to the next level.”

Short Intro Bio (Podcasts & Panels)

Dan Dawes is the Co-Founder & CEO of Response Labs, a four-time consecutive Inc. 5000 CRM and loyalty marketing agency headquartered in Baltimore’s Natty Boh Tower. Previously, he founded Congruent Media and led it to an acquisition by Havas. He also serves as an Advisor to the Tyus Bowser Foundation.

Executive Program Bio (Keynotes & Press)

Dan Dawes is a Baltimore-based serial entrepreneur and digital marketing executive with over 20 years of leadership building high-growth agencies. In 2003, he founded Congruent Media inside Baltimore’s iconic Natty Boh Tower, growing it to serve clients like Johns Hopkins University before its 2010 acquisition by Havas (Euro RSCG 4D Discovery). In 2014, Dan co-founded Response Labs, guiding enterprise brands including Ahold Delhaize, Outback Steakhouse, and Playa Bowls to turn first-party data into lasting loyalty systems. Under his leadership, Response Labs has earned four straight Inc. 5000 honors (2022–2025). Beyond agency life, Dan built the Maryland craft brand Dundalk Dan’s and advises the Tyus Bowser Foundation.
