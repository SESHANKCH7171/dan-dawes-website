The Journey

Two decades of building from Charm City.

Dan Dawes is a Baltimore-born entrepreneur and digital marketing veteran who believes technology only works when it serves real human relationships.

From founding Congruent Media in 2003 and steering it to an acquisition by global holding company Havas, to co-founding Response Labs in 2014 and returning to the iconic Natty Boh Tower, his career has been anchored in operational rigor, people-first culture, and an unwavering builder's mindset.

“The smartest brands in 2026 understand that loyalty isn't a program. It's a system. It powers every engagement, every campaign, and every decision about how to treat customers.”
— Dan Dawes, Response Labs Perspectives
2003 Brewer's Hill

Founded Congruent Media

Launched an interactive marketing and design shop on the 2nd floor of the newly renovated Natty Boh Tower in Baltimore. Built long-term digital engagements for Johns Hopkins University, Speakman Co., Metal Roofing Alliance, Hurricane Grill & Wings, and Pollard Banknote.

AUGUST 2010 Adweek Milestone

Acquisition by Havas / Euro RSCG 4D

Steered Congruent Media (20-person team) through its successful acquisition by global advertising holding giant Havas, joining Euro RSCG 4D Discovery as Senior Vice President alongside co-founder Jeffrey Rudolf.

Read the Adweek Announcement ↗
JANUARY 2014 Baltimore ETC Incubator

Co-Founded Response Labs

Teamed up with Brian E. Langston (CTO) and Jeffrey Rudolf to launch Response Labs in Baltimore's Emerging Technology Center (ETC). Set a bold mandate: build enterprise-grade CRM, email, and customer loyalty infrastructure with a steadfast focus on craft, transparency, and data integrity.

2016 – 2019 Consumer Craft Venture

Launched Dundalk Dan's Beef Jerky

Turned a homemade office snack into a recognized Maryland craft brand across 4 flavors, 60+ regional retailers, and Amazon. Earned an official citation from the Comptroller of Maryland — living proof of an instinctive builder who understands product, supply chain, and brand affinity from scratch.

Featured in Technical.ly Baltimore ↗
AUGUST 2022 Full-Circle Homecoming

Return to The Natty Boh Tower

Having outgrown the 600-sq-ft ETC incubator, Dan relocated Response Labs HQ into Brewer's Hill's landmark Natty Boh Tower (Suite 500) — the exact building where his agency journey started nearly two decades earlier.
