## 3. Signature Interactive Artifact: "Signal From Noise" Wave Canvas

> **Why NOT a rotating 3D cube:** Rotating cubes look generic and distract from an executive's credibility.  
> **What to build instead:** A lightweight, silky-smooth HTML5 `<canvas>` backdrop inside the dark Hero section called **"The Loyalty Signal Wave"**.

### Technical Specification for the Interactive Wave
- Render 3 overlapping, ultra-thin translucent sine/Bezier wave ribbons flowing gently across the Deep Harbor Navy hero background (`#0E1726`) in Amber Gold (`rgba(200, 138, 46, 0.35)`), Signal Teal (`rgba(30, 127, 116, 0.30)`), and Soft White (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).
- Scatter ~28 subtle, low-opacity data-point dots along the wave paths.
- **Subtle Mouse Interaction:** Moving the cursor softly modulates wave amplitude and pulls nearby dots into gentle harmonic alignment — a quiet visual metaphor for *"Make Every Message Matter"* (turning scattered customer data into a clear signal).
- **Performance & Accessibility:** 60fps `requestAnimationFrame`, zero external heavy 3D libraries, automatically pauses or softens under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
